## Professional Experience

### [JOB TITLE] - [COMPANY] ([Month Year] - [Month Year or present])
[City, Country]

**Title:** which title to use outbound, if the one on the contract differs from the one the work
matched. Pick one and use it everywhere, so the CV, LinkedIn and any form agree.

**Framing rule:** if the product sits in a niche recruiters do not parse, write down the
vocabulary to translate into and the plain answer to give when asked. A translation is not a
concealment; a draft that cannot say plainly what the product was is the failure mode.

**Company context:** [what it does, headcount, team shape, who you reported to.] Scale changes
how a claim reads: "sole QA at a 14-person company" and "one of nine QAs" are different jobs.

**Products:** [what you actually worked on, with the platforms and the surface area.]

- One bullet per thing you owned. Lead with scope, then the number.
- Numbers go in with their unit and their boundary: which product, which period, counted how.
  "490 test cases across 7 suites, scoped to one product" survives a follow-up question;
  "hundreds of test cases" does not.
- **Meta-rules live as sub-bullets under the claim they govern.** Anything that must never reach
  a document (a client name under NDA, an internal defect, a tool you are not proud of) gets a
  sub-bullet saying so, plus what to say instead if an interviewer asks. `/apply` reads these.
- Where a figure does not exist, say so. An absent number is a prompt to go count it, not a
  licence to estimate one.
