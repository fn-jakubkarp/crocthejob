# Professional Record

Tell the story of your career here. Everything you can remember. No structure, no order, no
polished sentences.

The first pass will be a mess. That is what it is supposed to be. Then ask Claude to sort it out,
add whatever you remembered while reading it back, and come to it again later. This document gets
written several times, not once.

Whatever is in here is what every CV and interview answer later gets built from. Whatever is not
in here gets guessed at instead.

Nobody sees this file, so write it in the language you think in.

If you are staring at a blank page:

- what you actually did in each job, not the CV version
- what nobody asked you to do
- what you abandoned, and why you stopped
- what you cannot do
- what went wrong
- where you want to end up
